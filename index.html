What the handoff should settle

  1. Verify the customer task in the market before adapting a campaign asset.
  2. Choose a channel based on evidence and available delivery capacity.
  3. Have a local reviewer check language, cultural fit, and unsupported claims.
  4. Agree a measurement plan and owner before rollout; do not promise a search or sales result from the brief.
