A Customer Found the Shop Closed: Holiday-Hours Handoff

A customer writes, “The listing said the shop was open, but the door was locked.” Start with the date, branch and local time of the visit. A holiday closure can be correct for one shop even while another branch keeps its regular hours.

Separate the public reply from the hours correction

If the date was an exception, check the special hours for that date and branch. If the shop now closes every Tuesday, check its regular weekly hours. Google’s business-hours instructions distinguish the two. Replacing every Tuesday with “closed” to fix one holiday would introduce a second error.

When choosing Rankgasmo review management services, check which work you are buying. Its reply pack provides fifteen drafts for approval and manual posting. Its local profile tune-up is an improvement plan. Neither an approved reply nor a recommendation in a plan proves the public hours changed. Assign someone with access to the correct branch to make and check that edit.

Prepare the branch handoff

Use the shop name and public hours only. Once generated, the note can be copied into your team’s task list.

Hours to verify

Before replying, look up the branch as a customer and confirm the date shown. If the public hours are still wrong, acknowledge the customer’s experience and describe only the action already taken. Save “we corrected the listing” for the point when the correction is visible.
