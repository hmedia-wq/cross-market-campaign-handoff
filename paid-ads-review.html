What the review does—and does not—include

Keep the audit separate from buying media or managing campaigns. Rankgasmo's current service menu describes a one-off paid ads account audit and tracking checklist; it explicitly separates ad budget and ongoing management. The displayed service amount is a proposed planning estimate, not an approved tariff or purchase offer. Ask for a written final scope and fee before payment.

The handoff should identify the event, the test evidence, unresolved measurement gaps, the landing-page owner, and the next review date. Do not call a campaign successful on the basis of a dashboard screenshot alone.
